ABOUT THE ATTORNEY

Atty. Renan A. Nadado

Attorney at Law

Atty. Renan A. Nado is a legal professional based in Bacolod City, Negros Occidental.

He completed his elementary education at Dona Emilia Jaime Garcia Elementary School and his secondary education at Negros Occidental High School.

He continued his studies at La Salle Bacolod, earning a degree in Bachelor of Secondary Education major in Mathematics.

He later pursued legal education at the University of Recoletos Bacolod and successfully passed the Bar Examination in 2023.
